Engine recording used by the car sound
======================================

"Import car revs on Chassis Dyno with Turbo.wav" by editboy23, on Freesound
https://freesound.org/people/editboy23/sounds/496171/

Licence: Creative Commons 0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
CC0 asks for no credit; it is given here and in the app's About sheet as a courtesy.

What it is: a turbocharged Toyota Supra revved on a chassis dyno (the page says no more than that). It is a road-car
engine, not a 2026 Formula 1 power unit, and the app says so.

What UNSEEN did to it (engine.flac, engine.ts; reproducible with scripts/sound/prepare-engine.py):
  - started from Freesound's public high-quality preview of the sound (a lossy mp3 of the original upload);
  - mixed it to mono, kept 11.0 s to the end of the take, normalised the peak, and stored it as lossless FLAC, so
    there is no second lossy encode;
  - tracked the engine's firing frequency through the take from its spectrogram and stored where in the recording the
    engine is doing each frequency (the maps in engine.ts): the hard pull is the "power" voice, the fall after it,
    which ends in the take's real idle, is the "coast" voice.
The app plays the recording by position in it, at its own speed, never sped up or slowed down to change pitch
(src/app/sound/scrub.ts). Nothing is synthesised: if this file is missing the engine is silent.
